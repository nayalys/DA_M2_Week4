### Key findings taking shape
- **Driver inattention** is the #1 identifiable cause — distracted driving is a policy target
- **Afternoon rush hour** (4–6 PM) is consistently the most dangerous window
- **2020 shows a clear dip** — COVID lockdowns reduced traffic and crashes significantly
- **Severity proportions are similar** across boroughs — this is not a borough-specific problem

### Limitations I'm aware of
- ~30% of records have no borough — geographic analysis is incomplete
- Contributing factors rely on officer reporting — subjective and inconsistent
- Raw crash counts don't account for population or vehicle miles traveled
